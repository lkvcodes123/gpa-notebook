Grading scale & how it works

GPA is total grade points × course credits, divided by total graded credits. Regular courses get no bonus; Honors adds 0.5 and AP / IB adds 1.0 when weighting is on. An F always earns zero. P, W, I and blank grades are excluded.

Overall GPA follows each semester’s weighting setting. Unweighted GPA always excludes bonuses. Your school’s policy may differ.
