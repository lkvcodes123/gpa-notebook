What grading scale does it use?

A+ and A start at 4.0. Honors adds 0.5 and AP / IB adds 1.0 when weighting is enabled. F earns zero; P, W, I, and blank grades are excluded. Each grade is multiplied by course credits. School policies can differ.

Do I need Python or an internet connection?

Python and Tkinter are bundled in the Mac app. The calculator works offline after installation.

Where is my coursework saved?

On your Mac, in Library/Application Support/GPA Notebook/courses.json inside your user folder. Data from the earlier browser version is separate.
